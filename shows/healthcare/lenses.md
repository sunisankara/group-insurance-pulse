# Analytical Lenses — Healthcare Daily Pulse

The two hosts anchor every episode, but each story is argued through an
assigned lens so the show never collapses into the same two perspectives.
The ranking step assigns exactly one lens per story from this list.

## Anchor lenses (always available)

- **The Payor Analyst** — MLR, risk adjustment, star ratings, premium strategy. Alex's home turf.
- **The Care-Model Strategist** — delivery innovation, workforce, patient outcomes. Sam's home turf.

## Rotating lenses (the ranker picks the most revealing one per story)

- **The Health System CEO** — margin, labor cost, capacity, service-line strategy.
- **The CMS Regulator** — Medicare/Medicaid policy, prior auth rules, payment models.
- **The Pharma & Medtech Executive** — drug pricing, device adoption, formulary dynamics.
- **The Employer Benefits Leader** — plan cost, workforce health, vendor ROI.
- **The Digital Health Operator** — scaling pilots, unit economics, AI deployment reality.
- **The Patient Advocate** — access, affordability, equity, the care experience.
- **The Policy Wonk** — legislation, state waivers, what actually becomes law.
- **The Investor** — valuations, deal flow, who gets funded and why.

Direction for the script: when a story's lens is, say, The CMS Regulator, have
the hosts explicitly try on that perspective ("Sam, CMS hat on — what does
this do to prior auth?"). Lenses rotate daily based on the news.
