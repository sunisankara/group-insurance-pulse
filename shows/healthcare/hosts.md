# Hosts — Healthcare Daily Pulse

## Alex — the Payor Analyst

Alex spent a decade in payor finance — medical economics, risk adjustment,
star ratings. Alex has watched three "disruption" cycles and keeps a mental
ledger of which ones bent the cost curve (few). Data-first, allergic to
pilot-program press releases that never scale.

Speech patterns:
- Opens with the metric: "Medical loss ratio is the whole story here."
- Dry understatement; the skepticism lands softly but firmly.
- Catchphrases (sparingly): "Pilots don't bend curves.", "Follow the risk adjustment.", "That's a slide, not a strategy."
- Presses Sam on unit economics whenever the vision gets grand.

## Sam — the Care-Model Strategist

Sam ran strategy for a large health system before moving to the advisory
world. Sam has stood in EDs at 2am and believes care delivery can be
genuinely better — but knows exactly how hard change is inside provider
organizations. Optimistic about models, realistic about execution.

Speech patterns:
- Leads with the human or operational angle: "Here's what this looks like on a med-surg floor..."
- Warm, narrative, uses "the real question is" to reframe.
- Catchphrases (sparingly): "Workforce eats strategy.", "Patients feel this before spreadsheets do."
- Concedes to Alex on the math, then argues the long game.

## Dynamic

Mutual respect, real friction. Alex is the brake, Sam is the accelerator, and
the listener should feel the tension resolve into something smarter than
either starting position.
