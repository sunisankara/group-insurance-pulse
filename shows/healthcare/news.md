# News Verticals — Healthcare Daily Pulse

Audience: senior leaders (C-suite, EVPs, SVPs) across US healthcare — payors,
providers, and health tech.

## Primary targets to watch

Major national payors (UnitedHealthcare, Elevance, CVS/Aetna, Cigna, Humana,
Centene, Molina), large health systems (HCA, CommonSpirit, Ascension, Kaiser
Permanente, Intermountain), CMS/HHS, leading health tech (Epic, Oracle Health),
and disruptive entrants (Amazon, Walmart Health alumni, venture-backed care
models).

## Topics

Payor business (MLR, risk adjustment, star ratings, MA), provider economics
(labor, capacity, service lines), M&A and restructuring, CMS regulatory
rulings, major health tech deployments, employer health costs.

## Story types to prioritize

- M&A, restructuring, and market exits
- CMS/HHS regulatory rulings and payment model changes
- Earnings signals: MLR movement, membership shifts, margin commentary
- Major health tech deployments with measurable outcomes
- Employer and broker/consultant moves affecting plan design
- Policy and legislation with a real path to enactment

## Ranking rubric (used by the ranker, weights as guidance)

1. Business/financial impact (30%) — moves revenue, margin, or cost curve
2. Strategic surprise (25%) — unexpected, first-of-kind, or contrarian
3. Regulatory/policy weight (20%) — changes payment or operating rules
4. Market breadth (15%) — affects many organizations, not one niche player
5. Patient/member relevance (10%) — changes access, affordability, or experience

Deprioritize: pilot announcements without scale evidence, vendor fluff
without hard numbers, non-US stories unless they directly affect US healthcare.
