# Evergreen Topics — Healthcare Daily Pulse

Practical briefing topics for thin-news days. When the day's fresh news
slate holds only one or two stories, the episode closes with one of these
segments instead of padding the news or repeating a recent story. Topics
rotate by day of year; each segment is built from a fresh search-grounded
brief (named systems, payers, reported numbers, concrete practices),
never from generic advice. Edit this list freely — the engine reads the
bold topic names and their angles.

- **AI in clinical documentation** — ambient scribes and documentation automation: adoption numbers, what systems report on clinician time saved, and where the ROI argument breaks down.
- **Prior authorization automation** — the payer and provider state of play: gold-carding, real-time benefit tools, regulatory pressure, and what is actually reducing denial and delay volume.
- **GLP-1 cost management** — how payers and employers are responding to GLP-1 demand: coverage design, step therapy, compounding policy, and the numbers behind the decisions.
- **Value-based care contracts that actually work** — the contract structures, attribution methods, and care-management investments that separate performing arrangements from press releases.
- **Hospital margin recovery** — the operational levers health systems are pulling: throughput, length of stay, labor mix, service-line pruning, with reported results.
- **Site-of-care shifts** — moving infusion, imaging, and surgical volume to lower-cost settings: who is moving what, the savings claimed, and the friction with physicians and patients.
- **Workforce models under pressure** — staffing models, float pools, virtual nursing, and retention economics at systems facing permanent labor-cost resets.
- **Payer-provider data sharing in practice** — interoperability beyond the mandate: admission-discharge-transfer feeds, risk data exchange, and where collaboration measurably changes outcomes or cost.
