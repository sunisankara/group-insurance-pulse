# Evergreen Topics — Group Insurance Daily Pulse

Practical briefing topics for thin-news days. When the day's fresh news
slate holds only one or two stories, the episode closes with one of these
segments instead of padding the news or repeating a recent story. Topics
rotate by day of year; each segment is built from a fresh search-grounded
brief (named companies, reported numbers, concrete practices), never
from generic advice. Edit this list freely — the engine reads the bold
topic names and their angles.

- **Growing group life insurance sales** — what actually moves employer-paid and voluntary life uptake: participation drivers, guaranteed-issue design, and the broker playbooks carriers report working.
- **Growing disability sales (STD and LTD)** — how carriers and brokers raise short- and long-term disability participation: enrollment positioning, employer funding models, and integration with absence management.
- **AI in claims operations** — where group carriers are actually deploying AI in disability and life claims, what it does to cycle times and accuracy, and where humans still decide.
- **Emerging distribution: embedded benefits and ben-admin platforms** — how benefits-administration platforms, payroll embeds, and marketplaces are changing who sells group benefits and how carriers plug in.
- **Enrollment engagement that works** — decision support, communication timing, and enrollment mechanics that measurably raise participation, with named employer and carrier examples.
- **PFML as a sales wedge** — state paid family and medical leave mandates as an opening for absence and disability conversations: how carriers package compliance plus supplemental coverage.
- **Underwriting automation in group benefits** — evidence and experience requirements, speed-to-quote, and where automated underwriting is winning or losing business.
- **Voluntary benefits cross-sell** — worksite and supplemental products (accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity) and the cross-sell motions that raise take-up without burning broker goodwill.
- **The renewal and retention playbook** — what top carriers and brokers do in the 120 days before renewal to keep group business: early data, rate-story discipline, and service recovery.
- **Broker consolidation and what it means for carriers** — agency M&A, private-equity roll-ups, and how distribution strategy changes when the broker shelf keeps shrinking.
