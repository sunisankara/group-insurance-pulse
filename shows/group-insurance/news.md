# News Verticals — Group Insurance Daily Pulse

Audience: senior leaders (C-suite, EVPs, SVPs) at major US group insurance carriers.

## Primary targets (competitors to watch)

MetLife, Prudential Financial, Lincoln Financial, Unum, The Hartford, Sun Life,
Guardian Life, Principal Financial, Mutual of Omaha, Voya Financial, Reliance
Matrix, OneAmerica, Securian Financial, Standard Insurance Company (The Standard),
Aflac, USAble Life, Dearborn Group, New York Life Group Benefit Solutions.

## Priority watch

- **New York Life Group Benefit Solutions (NYL GBS)** — cover at least twice
  a week. New York Life is a MUTUAL insurer: it has no stock, no public
  earnings, and no SEC filings, so it will never surface through
  earnings-driven coverage — do not treat the absence of earnings signals
  as an absence of news. For NYL GBS, product launches, partnerships,
  distribution deals, leadership moves, platform and technology rollouts,
  and published sales/results figures are first-class news. If the last
  48 hours hold nothing, look back over the last 7 days for an uncovered
  development (the recall prompt enforces this window).

## Topics

Group Life, Disability (STD/LTD), Accident, Critical Illness, Accidental
Injury, Hospital Indemnity, Employee/Voluntary Benefits.

## Story types to prioritize

- M&A, partnerships, and market exits
- Regulatory changes (ERISA, PFML mandates, DOL rules, state DOI actions)
- Earnings signals: loss ratios, premium growth, margin commentary
- Technology deployments in claims, enrollment, billing, and commissions
- Broker/consultant moves (Mercer, WTW, Aon) and TPA news
- Product launches with real differentiation (not me-too riders)

## Ranking rubric (used by the ranker, weights as guidance)

1. Carrier P&L impact (30%) — moves revenue, margin, or risk materially
2. Strategic surprise (25%) — unexpected, first-of-kind, or contrarian
3. Regulatory/policy weight (20%) — changes the compliance or cost landscape
4. Market breadth (15%) — affects many carriers, not one niche player
5. Distribution relevance (10%) — changes what brokers sell or how

Deprioritize: incremental press-release fluff, vendor announcements without
hard numbers, non-US stories unless they directly affect US carriers.
