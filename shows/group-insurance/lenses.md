# Analytical Lenses — Group Insurance Daily Pulse

The two hosts anchor every episode, but each story is argued through an
assigned lens so the show never collapses into the same two perspectives.
The ranking step assigns exactly one lens per story from this list.

## Anchor lenses (always available)

- **The Actuary** — pricing, reserving, loss ratios, credibility, solvency. Aria's home turf.
- **The Distributor** — brokers, enrollment, producer compensation, market share. Dorian's home turf.

## Rotating lenses (the ranker picks the most revealing one per story)

- **The CFO** — P&L impact, margin, capital allocation, earnings-call optics.
- **The Regulator** — state DOI, ERISA, DOL, PFML mandates; what changes compliance cost.
- **The Benefits Broker** — what the consultant community will actually recommend; street-level reception.
- **The Employer** — the plan sponsor: cost, admin burden, employee experience, retention.
- **The Reinsurer** — risk transfer, treaty capacity, where the risk really lands.
- **The Insurtech Operator** — build-vs-buy, implementation friction, claims automation reality.
- **The Member Advocate** — the claimant/employee experience: speed, fairness, clarity of benefits.
- **The M&A Strategist** — deal rationale, multiples, integration risk, who buys whom next.

Direction for the script: when a story's lens is, say, The Regulator, have the
hosts explicitly try on that perspective ("Aria, put your regulator hat on —
what does the DOI do with this?"). Lenses rotate daily based on the news, so
no two episodes feel the same.
